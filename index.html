Sources & reference bands

Every band tag is the source's own published wording, quoted verbatim, with the date we last verified it. vitalsheet renders these exactly; it never re-interprets them. The home-measurement technique lines below appear here only — they are never woven into any calculation.

Not a medical device. vitalsheet is an informational log book only, not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for professional care. It records the numbers you type, computes their plain arithmetic averages, and shows published reference bands verbatim with a verified-on date. It never answers “is this bad?” and gives no diagnosis, verdict or trend judgement. The ADA states glycemic targets are individualized; reference bands are dated population guidelines.
